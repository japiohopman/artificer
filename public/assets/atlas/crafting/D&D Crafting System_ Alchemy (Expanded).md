# D&D Crafting System: Alchemy (Expanded)

This section details the Alchemy crafting system, now expanded to utilize a broader range of ingredients for brewing potions, distilling oils, creating bombs, and preparing various alchemical reagents. Alchemy focuses on the transformation of natural and monster-derived ingredients into consumable or deployable effects.

## 1. Core Alchemy Mechanics

Alchemy primarily utilizes **Herbs**, **Minerals**, **Monster Glands/Fluids**, **Elemental Essences**, and specialized **Arcane Reagents** as primary ingredients. The crafting process is similar to general crafting but emphasizes precision and knowledge of chemical reactions.

### 1.1 The Alchemical Process

1.  **Ingredient Gathering:** Players gather herbs, minerals, and monster parts suitable for alchemical use. The expanded material database offers significantly more options.
2.  **Recipe Acquisition:** Recipes can be found, purchased, or discovered through experimentation (requiring higher skill checks and potentially rare ingredients).
3.  **Preparation:** Ingredients often require specific preparation (grinding, boiling, distilling, purification) before they can be combined.
4.  **Skill Check:** An Intelligence (Alchemist's Supplies) check is made. The DC is influenced by the potion's rarity, complexity, and the volatility of its ingredients.
5.  **Time and Cost:** Alchemy typically requires less raw material cost than smithing but can be time-consuming due to delicate processes. Mundane components like vials, stoppers, and heating elements add to the cost. Rare alchemical reagents can significantly increase costs.
6.  **Result:** Success yields the desired alchemical item. Failure might result in a weaker version, a harmful concoction, or wasted ingredients.

## 3. Alchemical Item Types

*   **Potions:** Consumable liquids granting temporary buffs, healing, or special effects (e.g., Potion of Healing, Potion of Growth, Potion of Fire Breath).
*   **Oils/Salves:** Topical applications for weapons, armor, or skin, providing temporary enchantments or protections (e.g., Oil of Sharpness, Troll Fat Salve, Oil of Slipperiness).
*   **Poisons:** Applied to weapons or food, inflicting various debilitating effects (e.g., Basic Poison, Vermite Toxin, Black Dragon Blood Poison).
*   **Bombs/Concoctions:** Thrown or deployed items with area-of-effect damage or status effects (e.g., Alchemist's Fire, Smoke Bomb, Acid Flask).
*   **Reagents:** Ingredients used in other crafting systems or for ritualistic purposes (e.g., Fairy Dust for Arcane Crafting, Spirit Stone for enchantments).

## 4. Example Alchemy Recipes (Expanded)

### Recipe: Potion of Greater Healing
*   **Output:** Potion of Greater Healing (2d4 + 2 HP)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Blueglow Moss (Uncommon Herb) x2, Hydra Blood (Rare Monster Fluid) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Salt Crystal (Common Mineral) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Alchemist's Supplies
*   **Crafting DC:** 15 (Base 10 + 2 Uncommon + 3 Rare)
*   **Crafting Time:** 4 hours
*   **Crafting Cost:** 25 gp
*   **Description:** A potent healing draught that restores a significant amount of hit points.

### Recipe: Oil of Sharpness
*   **Output:** Oil of Sharpness (Weapon gains +1 to attack and damage for 1 hour)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Rust Monster Antenna (Uncommon Monster Part) x1, Vermite (Rare Mineral) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Pitch (Common Organic) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Alchemist's Supplies
*   **Crafting DC:** 17 (Base 10 + 2 Uncommon + 5 Rare)
*   **Crafting Time:** 8 hours
*   **Crafting Cost:** 75 gp
*   **Description:** A viscous oil that temporarily sharpens the edge of any bladed weapon.

### Recipe: Potion of Fire Breath (Red Dragon)
*   **Output:** Potion of Fire Breath (Exhale fire in a 15-foot cone, 3d6 Fire damage, Dex save for half)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Red Dragon Blood (Rare Monster Fluid) x1, Firestone (Uncommon Mineral) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Hiexel (Common Wood) x1 (for ignition properties)
*   **Required Tools:** Alchemist's Supplies
*   **Crafting DC:** 16 (Base 10 + 5 Rare + 1 Uncommon)
*   **Crafting Time:** 6 hours
*   **Crafting Cost:** 60 gp
*   **Description:** A fiery concoction that allows the drinker to unleash a blast of dragonfire.

### Recipe: Potion of Water Breathing
*   **Output:** Potion of Water Breathing (Can breathe underwater for 1 hour)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Aboleth Mucus (Rare Monster Fluid) x1, Water Elemental Essence (Uncommon Elemental) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Coral (Uncommon Stone) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Alchemist's Supplies
*   **Crafting DC:** 15 (Base 10 + 5 Rare)
*   **Crafting Time:** 4 hours
*   **Crafting Cost:** 40 gp
*   **Description:** A clear, effervescent liquid that grants the ability to breathe water.

## 5. Integration with Material Database

The expanded `material_database_200.csv` now provides a significantly richer pool of ingredients for alchemy. This includes various Dragon Bloods (Red, Blue, Black, Green, White), additional monster fluids (Gelatinous Cube Essence, Rust Monster Blood), and exotic minerals (Plutite, Necratite, Vermite) to create a vast array of alchemical products. The system is designed to leverage these diverse materials for unique and powerful effects.
