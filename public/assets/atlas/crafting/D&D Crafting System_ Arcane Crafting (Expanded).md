# D&D Crafting System: Arcane Crafting (Expanded)

This section details the Arcane Crafting system, now significantly expanded to enable players to create a wider array of magical items such as scrolls, wands, staves, and enchanted focuses. Arcane crafting relies on infusing mundane items with magical energy using rare materials and intricate rituals, leveraging the newly expanded material database.

## 1. Core Arcane Crafting Mechanics

Arcane crafting primarily uses **Exotic Metals**, **Magical Woods**, **Mystic Stones/Crystals**, **Monster Arcane Parts**, and **Elemental Essences** as primary ingredients. The process is often complex, requiring specialized knowledge, a strong connection to magical energies, and precise execution.

### 1.1 The Arcane Crafting Process

1.  **Ingredient Gathering:** Players acquire materials with inherent magical properties or those that can be imbued with magic. The expanded material database offers a richer selection of magically resonant components.
2.  **Recipe Acquisition:** Arcane recipes are typically found in ancient tomes, learned from powerful spellcasters, discovered through dangerous magical research, or bestowed by otherworldly entities.
3.  **Preparation & Infusion:** Materials may need to be prepared (e.g., purified, carved, inscribed, attuned) and then infused with magical energy through elaborate rituals, prolonged spellcasting, or direct channeling of magical power.
4.  **Skill Check:** An Intelligence (Arcana) check is made, often with proficiency in a relevant tool (e.g., Calligrapher's Supplies for scrolls, Jeweler's Tools for focuses, Woodcarver's Tools for wands/staves). The DC is determined by the item's power, rarity, and the complexity of the enchantment, with higher DCs for legendary and artifact-tier items.
5.  **Time and Cost:** Arcane crafting is often the most time-consuming and expensive form of crafting, requiring exceptionally rare components, costly inks and reagents, and significant magical energy (sometimes represented by expended spell slots, temporary magical exhaustion, or even sacrifices).
6.  **Result:** Success yields a magical item. Failure can result in a mundane item, a cursed item, a wild magic surge, a magical backlash (potentially harming the crafter or their surroundings), or the complete destruction of valuable components.

## 3. Arcane Item Types

*   **Scrolls:** Single-use items that allow a spellcaster to cast a spell they might not normally know or have prepared (e.g., Scroll of Fireball, Scroll of Protection, Scroll of Planar Binding).
*   **Wands/Staves:** Spellcasting foci that can store and cast spells, often with limited charges or unique properties (e.g., Wand of Magic Missiles, Staff of the Woodlands, Staff of the Magi).
*   **Enchanted Armor/Weapons:** Mundane items imbued with magical properties (e.g., +1 Longsword, Shield of Resistance, Dragon Scale Armor of Fire Resistance).
*   **Amulets/Rings/Foci:** Jewelry or small objects that grant passive magical benefits, resistances, enhance spellcasting, or provide unique abilities (e.g., Amulet of Health, Ring of Protection, Orb of Dragonkind).
*   **Infusions:** Temporary magical enhancements applied to items, often by Artificers, granting a specific magical property for a duration (e.g., Enhanced Weapon, Repulsion Shield).
*   **Ritual Components:** Consumable or reusable items specifically required for powerful magical rituals (e.g., Dracolich Bone Dust for Necromantic rituals, Sphinx Sand for temporal magic).

## 4. Example Arcane Crafting Recipes (Expanded)

### Recipe: Scroll of Fireball (3rd Level)
*   **Output:** Scroll of Fireball
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Fine Vellum (Common Material) x1, Red Dragon Blood (Rare Monster Fluid) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Firestone (Uncommon Elemental) x1, Orichalcum Dust (Exotic Metal) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Calligrapher's Supplies, Arcana proficiency
*   **Crafting DC:** 15 (Base 10 + 5 Rare)
*   **Crafting Time:** 1 day
*   **Crafting Cost:** 100 gp (for inks, rare pigments)
*   **Description:** A single-use scroll containing the powerful *Fireball* spell.

### Recipe: Wand of Magic Missiles
*   **Output:** Wand of Magic Missiles (7 charges, regains 1d6+1 daily)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Blueleaf (Uncommon Wood) x1, Orium (Exotic Metal) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Quartz (Common Mystic Stone) x1, Fairy Dust (Rare Reagent) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Woodcarver's Tools, Arcana proficiency
*   **Crafting DC:** 17 (Base 10 + 2 Uncommon + 5 Rare)
*   **Crafting Time:** 5 days
*   **Crafting Cost:** 300 gp
*   **Description:** A slender wand that can unleash bolts of pure magical force.

### Recipe: Amulet of Protection +1
*   **Output:** Amulet of Protection +1 (Grants +1 bonus to AC and Saving Throws)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Silver (Common Metal) x1, Spirit Stone (Very Rare Mystic Stone) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Moonsilver (Exotic Metal) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Jeweler's Tools, Arcana proficiency
*   **Crafting DC:** 20 (Base 10 + 8 Very Rare + 2 for complex enchantment)
*   **Crafting Time:** 10 days
*   **Crafting Cost:** 1000 gp
*   **Description:** A finely crafted amulet imbued with protective magic.

### Recipe: Staff of the Woodlands
*   **Output:** Staff of the Woodlands (Requires attunement by a Druid or Ranger; acts as a +2 quarterstaff; can cast various plant-related spells)

*   **Crafting Time:** 20 days
*   **Crafting Cost:** 5000 gp
*   **Description:** A staff carved from ancient, living wood, pulsating with natural magic.
