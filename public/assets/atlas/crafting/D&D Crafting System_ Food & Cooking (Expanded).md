# D&D Crafting System: Food & Cooking (Expanded)

This section outlines the Food & Cooking crafting system, now capable of utilizing a much broader range of ingredients to prepare meals, create monster cuisine, and brew beverages that provide temporary buffs, healing, or other beneficial effects. This system emphasizes resourcefulness and the utilization of organic materials, including a wider variety of monster parts.

## 1. Core Cooking Mechanics

Cooking primarily uses **Raw Food**, **Herbs**, **Monster Meat/Organs**, and **Spices/Flavorings** as ingredients. The process involves combining these ingredients with culinary tools to create edible items.

### 1.1 The Cooking Process

1.  **Ingredient Gathering:** Players acquire raw food (fruits, vegetables, grains), a wider array of herbs, and various monster parts suitable for consumption. The expanded material database offers many new culinary possibilities.
2.  **Recipe Acquisition:** Recipes can be learned from NPCs, discovered through exploration, or experimented with (requiring higher skill checks and potentially rare ingredients).
3.  **Preparation:** Ingredients may require cleaning, chopping, tenderizing, or other basic preparation before cooking. Some monster parts might require special handling.
4.  **Skill Check:** A Wisdom (Cook's Utensils) or Dexterity (Chef's Tools) check is made. The DC is influenced by the complexity of the dish, the rarity of its ingredients, and the desired effect.
5.  **Time and Cost:** Cooking generally requires minimal monetary cost beyond basic spices and fuel, but can take time depending on the complexity of the meal. Simple meals might be quick, while elaborate feasts take hours. Rare spices or preservation methods can increase costs.
6.  **Result:** Success yields a prepared dish with beneficial effects. Failure might result in a bland meal (no effect), a spoiled dish (minor negative effect), or wasted ingredients.

## 2. Culinary Ingredient Categories

| Category | Examples (Expanded) | Primary Use | Associated Skills |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raw Food** | Candy, Dough, Salt, Honey, Sugar, Pepper, Fruits, Vegetables, Grains | Basic meals, snacks, preservation, flavor, base for complex dishes | Survival, Nature |
| **Herbs** | Basil, Bay Leaf, Juniper Berry, Marjoram, Mallow, Umber Leaf, Blueleaf (for flavor) | Flavoring, minor buffs, medicinal properties, aromatic components | Nature, Medicine |
| **Monster Meat/Organs** | Troll Fat, Raw Meat (from various beasts), Monster Eggs, Brood Fruit, Owlbear Beak (for broth), Dragon Tongue | Monster cuisine, powerful buffs, unique effects, specialized ingredients | Survival, Medicine |
| **Spices/Flavorings** | Pepper, Salt, Saffron, Cinnamon, Garlic, Exotic Spices | Enhancing taste, adding minor buffs, preservation | Survival, Investigation |

## 3. Food Item Types

*   **Meals:** Prepared dishes that provide temporary buffs to stats, resistances, or grant temporary hit points (e.g., Hearty Stew, Dragon Steak, Owlbear Goulash).
*   **Snacks:** Quick consumables for minor healing or small, short-duration buffs (e.g., Candied Spider Legs, Foraged Berries, Dried Dragon Meat).
*   **Beverages:** Brewed drinks that can restore spell slots, provide inspiration, or grant social advantages (e.g., Elven Wine, Dwarven Ale, Potion of Glibness (from Dragon Tongue)).
*   **Preserves:** Food items treated to last longer, useful for long journeys (e.g., Salted Meat, Dried Fruits, Pickled Monster Organs).

## 4. Example Cooking Recipes (Expanded)

### Recipe: Hearty Troll Stew
*   **Output:** Hearty Troll Stew (Restores 2d8 + Con modifier HP, grants 1d4 temporary HP for 1 hour)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Troll Fat (Uncommon Monster Part) x1, Raw Meat (Common Raw Food) x2
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Marjoram (Common Herb) x1, Salt (Common Raw Food) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Cook's Utensils
*   **Crafting DC:** 14 (Base 10 + 2 Uncommon + 2 for multiple ingredients)
*   **Crafting Time:** 1 hour
*   **Crafting Cost:** 5 gp
*   **Description:** A rich, savory stew made with regenerative troll fat, providing substantial nourishment and a temporary boost to vitality.

### Recipe: Candied Spider Legs
*   **Output:** Candied Spider Legs (Restores 1d4 HP, grants +1 to Dexterity saving throws for 10 minutes)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Giant Spider Leg (Common Monster Part) x2, Candy (Common Raw Food) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Sugar (Common Raw Food) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Cook's Utensils
*   **Crafting DC:** 11 (Base 10 + 1 for multiple ingredients)
*   **Crafting Time:** 30 minutes
*   **Crafting Cost:** 2 gp
*   **Description:** A surprisingly delicious and crunchy snack that provides a quick burst of agility.

### Recipe: Dragon Steak (Red Dragon)
*   **Output:** Dragon Steak (Grants Resistance to Fire damage for 1 hour)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Red Dragon Meat (Rare Monster Part) x1
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Pepper (Common Raw Food) x1, Basil (Common Herb) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Cook's Utensils
*   **Crafting DC:** 16 (Base 10 + 5 Rare + 1 for seasoning)
*   **Crafting Time:** 2 hours
*   **Crafting Cost:** 50 gp
*   **Description:** A rare and potent meal prepared from the flesh of a red dragon, imbuing the consumer with temporary fire resistance.

### Recipe: Owlbear Goulash
*   **Output:** Owlbear Goulash (Grants +1 to Strength checks for 30 minutes)
*   **Primary Ingredients:** Owlbear Meat (Uncommon Monster Part) x2, Root Vegetables (Common Raw Food) x3
*   **Secondary Ingredients:** Garlic (Common Spice) x1, Pine (Common Wood, for smoky flavor) x1
*   **Required Tools:** Cook's Utensils
*   **Crafting DC:** 13 (Base 10 + 2 Uncommon + 1 for multiple ingredients)
*   **Crafting Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes
*   **Crafting Cost:** 8 gp
*   **Description:** A hearty and filling stew made from robust owlbear meat, known to invigorate the consumer.

## 5. Integration with Material Database

The expanded `material_database_200.csv` now offers a significantly richer and more diverse selection of ingredients for the Food & Cooking system. This includes various monster meats (e.g., Owlbear Meat, Giant Spider Leg), additional herbs and spices, and unique organic components that can be transformed into beneficial culinary creations. The system is designed to encourage exploration and creative use of all available materials.
