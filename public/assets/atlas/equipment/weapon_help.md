# Weapon & Attack Guide

Weapons are categorized by weapon proficiencies, range, and damage dice.

### Weapon Properties
- **Finesse:** Allows using Dexterity modifier instead of Strength for attack and damage rolls.
- **Versatile:** Can be wielded with one or two hands, dealing higher damage when wielded with two hands.
- **Heavy:** Small creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls with heavy weapons.
- **Reach:** Extends melee attack range on the combat grid by 5 feet.
- **Thrown:** Can be thrown for ranged attacks using your Strength or Dexterity modifier.
