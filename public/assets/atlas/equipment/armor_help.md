# Armor & Defense Guide

Armor determines your base Armor Class (AC) and defensive capabilities.

### Armor Categories
1. **Light Armor:** Grants AC bonus + full Dexterity modifier (e.g., Leather Armor, Studded Leather).
2. **Medium Armor:** Grants AC bonus + Dexterity modifier (maximum +2) (e.g., Hide, Scale Mail, Breastplate).
3. **Heavy Armor:** Grants high base AC without Dexterity modifier; requires Strength score to wear without speed penalty (e.g., Ring Mail, Chain Mail, Plate).
4. **Shields:** Equipping a shield adds +2 to your total Armor Class.
