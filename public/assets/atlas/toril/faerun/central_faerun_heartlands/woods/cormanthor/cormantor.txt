

Cormanthor
The forest of Cormanthor, also known as The Elven Woods and The Northern Forest, was long the place of the elven empire of Cormanthyr. It is a large, dense forest comprising various species of plants, including some trees that can grow over 100 feet deep in the woods. Although Cormanthor can be considered one forest, it incorporates four different forests: the Elven Court, Semberholme, the Tangled Trees, and the Vale of Lost Voices. The first three roughly correspond to the old elven communities of the same names. The forest is also divided roughly according to tree type: the Rimwood is mainly made up of pine trees, runs along the edge of the forest and is the least dense part of it; the Midwood consists chiefly of white ash and beech trees, separates the Rimwood from the Starwood and accounts for roughly half of the total area of the forest; the Starwood is made up mostly of giant oaks and maple and is the densest part of the forest. Light rain serves the forest year-round, and the ground is usually moist, keeping humidity high. Winds cannot penetrate the thick, dense canopy, so the forest is calm at ground level. At the height of the summer, storms can come and go in just an hour. There are often patches of dense fog in the forest, especially near the northern Elvenflow. The fireflies in the forest can cause wild blazes, especially on clear summer nights, when they assemble in huge swarms.
