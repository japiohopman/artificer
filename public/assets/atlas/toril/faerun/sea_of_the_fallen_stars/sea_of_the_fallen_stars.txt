Sea of Fallen Stars
The Sea of Fallen Stars, also known as the Inner Sea, is the largest inland body of water in Faerûn, large enough to feel tidal effects. The vast majority of it is saltwater. It mixes with freshwater to become brackish near many of the coastal shallows, especially at inlets where rivers drained into the sea. The sea serves as a crucial trade way for the powers of central and northern Faerûn and beyond. There is a recognized territorial limit of ten miles offshore among all the nations on the Sea of Fallen Stars. Areas of sea further than ten miles from the nearest land are considered neutral territory. Any attacks on shipping within a country's waters are considered to be acts of war.
