Major Underdark Regions

Northwest Underdark
The Northdark: A region of the Upperdark located beneath the Sword Coast and the Savage Frontier, home to [[Menzoberranzan]].
The Labyrinth: A vast, complex network of tunnels and caverns in the Middledark beneath the Savage Frontier.
The Far Realm: A region in the Lowerdark where the influence of the Far Realm is strongest, leading to warped reality.
Araumycos: A massive, sentient fungal colony that fills a large portion of the Upper and Middledark beneath the High Forest.
The Sharplands: A jagged, dangerous region of the Lowerdark beneath the Savage Frontier.
The Buried Realms: Subterranean domains beneath the desert of Anauroch, formerly the heart of ancient Netheril.

Central Underdark
Southgrottos: Upperdark caverns beneath the Western Heartlands.
Netherese Caverns: Ancient caverns beneath the Heartlands containing remnants of Netheril's subterranean cities.
Old Shanatar: The ancient homeland of the shield dwarves beneath the Lands of Intrigue.
The Iron Route: A major trade route in the Middledark connecting several duergar cities.
The Deep Reach: A deep, unexplored region of the Lowerdark beneath the Sea of Fallen Stars.
The Glimmersea: A massive sunless sea in the Middledark beneath the Sea of Fallen Stars.
The Darklands: A region of the Lowerdark known for its extreme hostility and aberrant inhabitants.

Eastern Underdark
The Deep Wastes: A vast, desolate region of the Middledark beneath the Hordelands.
The Earthroot: A region of the Upper and Middledark beneath the Great Dale and Thesk.
The Boneyard: A massive cavern filled with the bones of ancient creatures in the Lowerdark.
The Ramparts of Night: A mountainous region of the Lowerdark beneath the Sunrise Mountains.
Deep Raurin: Caverns beneath the Raurin Desert, containing ancient, forgotten evils.
The Spawning Deep: A region of the sunless sea where aboleths and other aquatic horrors are born.

Southern Underdark
Great Bhaerynden: The massive rift and cavern system beneath the Shaar, former home of the drow and dwarves.
The Lightless Sea: A vast ocean in the Middledark beneath the Southern reaches of Faerun.
Serpents Deeps: Caverns beneath Chult and the Serpent Hills, infested with yuan-ti and nagas.
Telantiwar Deep: The ruins of an ancient drow empire in the Middledark beneath the Southern Shaar.
The Burning Rift: A volcanic region of the Lowerdark beneath the Land of the Lions.
