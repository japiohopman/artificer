# Choosing Your Background

Your background reveals where you came from, how you became an adventurer, and your place in the world prior to taking up the call.

---

### What Background Defines
* **Origin & Profession:** Your former life—whether a noble, criminal, hermit, or soldier.
* **Proficiencies:** Skills, tools, and languages learned through life experience.
* **Starting Gear:** Equipment and funds accumulated before adventuring.
* **Origin Privilege:** A unique background feature representing connections or social standing.
