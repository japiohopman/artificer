# The Three Cities: The Districts of Baldur's Gate

Baldur's Gate is not one city, but three distinct worlds existing within and without the same walls.

## I. The Upper City
The domain of the "Patriars"—the city's hereditary nobility.
*   **Landmarks:** The High Hall (seat of government), the Watch Citadel, and grand temples like the High House of Wonders.
*   **Atmosphere:** Clean, quiet, and exclusive. Only patriars and their servants are allowed here after dark.
*   **Security:** Protected by the **Watch**, an elite police force dedicated solely to the safety of the nobility.

## II. The Lower City
The bustling heart of trade and industry.
*   **Landmarks:** Gray Harbor, the Wide (the city's largest market), and the Elfsong Tavern.
*   **Atmosphere:** Loud, crowded, and vibrant. This is where the city's wealth is generated. The streets are a maze of shops, warehouses, and multi-story tenements.
*   **Security:** Patrolled by the **Flaming Fist**, a powerful mercenary company that serves as the city's standing army and police force.

## III. The Outer City
The lawless sprawl beyond the city walls.
*   **Landmarks:** Wyrm's Crossing (a bridge covered in buildings), the Calimshan District, and the Twin Songs.
*   **Atmosphere:** Chaotic and impoverished. It is home to the city's laborers, refugees, and those who cannot afford the taxes of the inner city.
*   **Security:** Virtually non-existent. The Flaming Fist only intervenes if the chaos threatens the city walls. The true law here is the **Guild**.

## Key Locations
*   **Gray Harbor:** One of the largest and busiest ports on the Sword Coast. Its deep waters can accommodate even the largest seafaring vessels.
*   **The Wide:** A massive open-air market in the Lower City where everything from exotic spices to enchanted trinkets can be found.
*   **Wyrm's Rock:** A formidable fortress situated on a rocky outcrop in the middle of the River Chionthar, controlling all traffic across the river.
