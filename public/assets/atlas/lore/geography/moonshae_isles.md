# The Seven Sisters: The Major Islands of the Moonshaes

The Moonshae archipelago consists of seven primary islands, each with its own unique climate, culture, and mysteries.

## I. Alaron
The seat of the High King and the cultural heart of the Folk.
*   **Capital:** Caer Calidyrr, a grand fortress-city overlooking the sea.
*   **Geography:** Fertile plains, rolling hills, and the majestic Fairheight Mountains.
*   **Key Site:** The *Derrynane Moonwell*, one of the most powerful and sacred sites on the islands.

## II. Gwynneth
A wild, mystical island where the influence of the Feywild is strongest.
*   **Inhabitants:** Primarily the Llewyrr (elves) and various fey creatures. The human population is small and lives mostly on the coasts.
*   **Geography:** Dense, ancient forests like the Myrloch Vale and the sprawling Lake Myrloch.
*   **Unique Feature:** The hidden elven city of *Chrysalis*, a place of song and silver light.

## III. Norland
The largest of the northern islands and the primary home of the Northlanders.
*   **Atmosphere:** Rugged, cold, and mountainous. Life here is a constant struggle against the elements.
*   **Culture:** A warrior society built on raiding, seafaring, and a deep respect for strength.
*   **Key Site:** The *Great Jarl's Hall*, where the Northlander kings gather to settle disputes.

## IV. Oman's Isle
A mountainous island that has long been a stronghold for giants and trolls.
*   **Geography:** Dominated by high peaks and deep, treacherous valleys.
*   **Conflict:** The Northlanders and giants are in a state of perpetual war for control of the island's iron mines.

## V. Snowdown
The southernmost island, once a lush paradise, now heavily exploited by foreign interests.
*   **Current State:** Occupied by the nation of **Amn**, which has stripped much of the island's timber and minerals.
*   **Atmosphere:** Tense and oppressed. The local Folk are in a state of quiet rebellion against their mainland masters.

## VI. Moray
The westernmost island, known for its dangerous bogs and the presence of the Black Blood tribe.
*   **Geography:** A landscape of treacherous fens and dark forests.
*   **Threat:** Home to the *Pack*, a group of lycanthropes who serve as a dark reflection of the Earth Mother's nature.
