# Cities of the Deep: Civilizations of the Underdark

The Underdark is dotted with isolated city-states, each a fortress of civilization in a sea of darkness.

## I. Menzoberranzan: The City of Spiders
The preeminent Drow city, Menzoberranzan is a place of breathtaking beauty and absolute cruelty. Built within a massive vault, its stalactites are carved into grand mansions and temples dedicated to **Lolth, the Spider Queen**.
*   **Culture:** A matriarchal society defined by house-to-house warfare, assassination, and the constant pursuit of divine favor.
*   **Atmosphere:** Opulent, dangerous, and filled with the tension of a thousand unsheathed daggers.

## II. Gracklstugh: The City of Blades
The industrial heart of the Underdark, this Duergar (Gray Dwarf) city is a place of smoke, fire, and the rhythmic pounding of a thousand hammers.
*   **Culture:** A society of master smiths and engineers, the Duergar are defined by their cynicism and mechanical efficiency. They produce the finest weapons and armor in the subterranean world.
*   **Atmosphere:** Choking with soot and heat, Gracklstugh is a monument to unyielding labor and iron-fisted order.

## III. Oryndoll: The City of Mind Flayers
Perhaps the most terrifying location in the Underdark, Oryndoll is a hive-city of **Mind Flayers (Illithids)**.
*   **Culture:** A collective consciousness centered around an **Elder Brain**. The inhabitants are obsessed with hoarding knowledge and experimenting on the "thralls" (slaves) they capture from the surface.
*   **Atmosphere:** Sterile, quiet, and thick with the heavy pressure of psionic radiation. It is a place where your thoughts are never truly your own.

## IV. Mantol-Derith: The Neutral Ground
Located roughly four miles deep, Mantol-Derith is a vital trading hub where the major races of the Underdark strike uneasy truces.
*   **Purpose:** Drow, Duergar, Svirfneblin (Deep Gnomes), and even surface-dwellers meet here to barter for rare resources like enchanted gems, exotic fungi, and slaves.
*   **Rules:** Violence is strictly forbidden within its walls, a rule enforced by magical wards and the mutual self-interest of its traders.

## V. Ooltul: The City of Beholders
A nation of Beholders located beneath Anauroch, secretly governed by a triumvirate of Phaerimm.
*   **Uniqueness:** Information is the primary currency here. The city uses a complex formula to determine the value of secrets, and power is measured by the depth of one's archives rather than the strength of one's army.
