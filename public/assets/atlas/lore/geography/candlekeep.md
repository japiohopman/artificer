# The Spires of Wisdom: The Geography of Candlekeep

Candlekeep is a sprawling complex of towers, courtyards, and subterranean vaults, all built upon a massive outcrop of volcanic rock. Its silhouette is one of the most recognizable landmarks on the Sword Coast.

## The Outer Ward: The Threshold
The first thing a visitor encounters are the **Main Gates**—three times the height of a man and forged from a strange, non-reflective black metal. This metal is immune to lightning and magical divination, acting as a shield for the library's secrets.

*   **The Court of Air:** Once through the gates, Seekers find themselves in a wide, cobblestone courtyard. This is as far as most visitors ever get. It contains the **Hearth**, a grand dining hall and social hub where Seekers can eat and sleep during their stay.
*   **The House of the Binder:** Here, the skilled scribes of the Avowed perform the painstaking work of copying non-magical texts for a hefty fee.
*   **The Pillars of Pedagogy:** A series of open-air classrooms where Master Readers provide instruction to acolytes and, occasionally, to visiting scholars.

## The Inner Ward: The Heart of the Library
Entry to the Inner Ward is restricted to the Avowed and those with special dispensation. It is separated from the Outer Ward by the **Emerald Door**, a massive portal of translucent green stone guarded by the Keeper of the Emerald Door.

*   **The Great Library:** A labyrinthine network of towers connected by stone bridges. Each tower is dedicated to a specific field of study—history, magic, geography, theology, and more. The shelves reach from floor to ceiling, and the air is thick with the scent of old parchment and beeswax.
*   **The Exaltation:** The tallest tower in Candlekeep, housing the private quarters of the Keeper of Tomes and the First Reader. Its peak is often shrouded in clouds.

## The Catacombs: Secrets Beneath the Stone
Beneath the visible towers lies a vast network of caverns and vaults.
*   **The Vaults:** This is where the most dangerous and rare items are kept—forbidden grimoires, cursed artifacts, and prophecies too volatile for the general collection.
*   **The Ghost Dragon's Lair:** The spectral remains of the silver dragon **Miirym** haunt these depths. Magically bound to protect the library even in death, she is a formidable guardian who can pass through walls to surprise intruders.

## The Sea of Swords
The fortress is built on the very edge of a cliff. Below, the Sea of Swords crashes against the volcanic rock. Secret sea caves are rumored to exist, providing hidden entrances for those brave or foolish enough to navigate the treacherous currents.
