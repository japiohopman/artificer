# The Nine Layers of Baator: A Topography of Terror

The Nine Hells is a plane of absolute order and infinite cruelty. It is structured like a funnel, with each layer descending deeper into the core of the plane, and each layer more hostile than the last.

## I. Avernus: The Blood-Soaked Gateway
**Ruler:** Archduchess Zariel (formerly Bel)
Avernus is a blasted wasteland of obsidian rocks and rivers of clotted blood. The sky is a bruised purple, streaked with fiery comets and the leathery wings of thousands of devils. This is the front line of the **Blood War**. Here, the legions of Hell meet the infinite hordes of the Abyss. It is a place of constant motion, where massive bronze citadels crawl across the landscape on iron treads, and the air is thick with the screams of the damned and the roar of infernal engines.

## II. Dis: The Iron Labyrinth
**Ruler:** Archduke Dispater
Below the wastes of Avernus lies Dis. It is a realm of jagged canyons and mountains of unyielding iron. At its center stands the **Iron City of Dis**, a metropolis of such scale that its walls stretch beyond the horizon. The city is a sprawling bureaucracy of iron and smoke, where every stone is heated to a blistering temperature. Dispater, the most paranoid of the Archdukes, rules from his Shuckrum Tower, watching every shadow for signs of betrayal.

## III. Minauros: The Endless Bog
**Ruler:** Archduke Mammon
Minauros is a foul, stinking swamp of oily water and acidic rain. It is a realm of decay and filth, where great cities of black stone slowly sink into the muck. Mammon, the personification of greed, rules from the Sinking City. Here, the air is filled with the sound of counting coins and the groans of those who sold everything for wealth, only to find it turning to lead in their hands.

## IV. Phlegethos: The Crucible of Fire
**Ruler:** Archduke Belial and Archduchess Fierna
A landscape of active volcanoes, rivers of liquid magma, and ash-choked winds. Phlegethos is the judicial heart of the Hells. In the city of Abriymoch, built within a dormant volcano, the diabolical courts settle disputes and hand out punishments. It is a place of searing heat and social intrigue, where the fires of the earth are matched only by the burning ambitions of its rulers.

## V. Stygia: The Frozen Sea
**Ruler:** Archduke Levistus
Stygia is a vast, dark ocean choked with icebergs and raked by freezing gales. The River Styx flows through this layer, its waters even more treacherous here. Levistus rules from within a massive iceberg, where he was imprisoned by Asmodeus for an ancient betrayal. He communicates with his subjects through telepathy and ice-mirrors, his frozen eyes seeing more than many realize.

## VI. Malbolge: The Infinite Slope
**Ruler:** Archduchess Glasya
Once a series of interconnected caverns, Malbolge was transformed by Asmodeus into an endless mountain slope. Boulders the size of cities tumble down its face in perpetual avalanches. Glasya, the daughter of Asmodeus, rules from a palace built into the ribs of a long-dead titan. It is a realm of treacherous footing and shifting loyalties, where the ground itself is as unstable as the politics.

## VII. Maladomini: The Ruined Archives
**Ruler:** Archduke Baalzebul
A wasteland of crumbling cities and abandoned monuments. Baalzebul, the Lord of the Flies, is obsessed with perfection and constantly orders new cities to be built, only to abandon them when they fail to meet his impossible standards. The air is thick with swarms of black flies, and the ground is littered with the records of every failure in the history of the Hells.

## VIII. Cania: The Citadel of Cold
**Ruler:** Archduke Mephistopheles
Cania is a realm of such absolute cold that it can freeze the soul. It is a place of jagged glaciers and towering spires of blue ice. Mephistopheles, the greatest wizard of the Hells, conducts his dark experiments here. His citadel, Mephistar, is a masterpiece of arcane architecture, where he seeks to unlock the secrets of hellfire and the ultimate power to rival Asmodeus.

## IX. Nessus: The Inner Sanctum
**Ruler:** Asmodeus
The final layer is a realm of dark pits and impossibly deep canyons. At the very bottom lies **Malsheem**, the Citadel of the Overlord. It is here that Asmodeus sits upon his throne, surrounded by his pit fiend generals. Nessus is the brain of the Hells, where every contract is recorded and every soul's fate is ultimately decided. It is a place of silence, shadows, and the crushing weight of absolute authority.
