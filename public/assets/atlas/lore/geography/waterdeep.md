# The Wards of Waterdeep: A Guide to the Districts

Waterdeep is divided into several distinct districts, each with its own laws, culture, and unique landmarks.

## I. The Castle Ward
The administrative and military heart of the city.
*   **Landmarks:** Castle Waterdeep, Piergeiron's Palace (The Open Lord's residence), and the Market.
*   **Atmosphere:** Busy, official, and heavily patrolled. This is where the laws are made and the city's defense is coordinated.
*   **Key Site:** *The Yawning Portal* – The world-famous tavern and gateway to Undermountain.

## II. The Sea Ward
The wealthiest and most prestigious district.
*   **Landmarks:** The Field of Triumph (coliseum), grand noble villas, and major temples like the House of Wonder.
*   **Atmosphere:** Opulent, exclusive, and beautiful. The streets are clean, the gardens are lush, and the fashion is cutting-edge.
*   **Unique Feature:** Many of the city's *Walking Statues* are located here or visible from its cliffs.

## III. The North Ward
The residential district for the upper-middle class and minor nobility.
*   **Landmarks:** Quiet parks, expensive boutiques, and the homes of successful merchants.
*   **Atmosphere:** Peaceful and refined. It lacks the ostentation of the Sea Ward but maintains a high standard of living.

## IV. The Trades Ward
The commercial engine of Waterdeep.
*   **Landmarks:** Court of the White Bull (a site of wild magic), countless guildhalls, and specialized markets.
*   **Atmosphere:** Chaotic, loud, and vibrant. This ward never sleeps, as commerce continues under the glow of magical streetlights.

## V. The Southern Ward
The district for commoners, laborers, and travelers.
*   **Landmarks:** The Waymoot (magical signpost), caravan stables, and modest inns.
*   **Atmosphere:** Down-to-earth and bustling with the energy of the road. It is the primary entry point for land-based trade.

## VI. The Dock Ward
The roughest and most dangerous part of the city.
*   **Landmarks:** The harbor, countless warehouses, and infamous taverns like the *Muleskull Tavern*.
*   **Atmosphere:** Gritty, salty, and lawless. Smuggling is a way of life here, and the City Watch rarely patrols deep into its narrowest alleys.

## VII. The City of the Dead
A vast, walled cemetery that serves as a public park.
*   **Landmarks:** Elaborate mausoleums, the *Ahghairon's Statue*, and hidden portals.
*   **Atmosphere:** Serene and beautiful by day; eerie and potentially haunted by night. It is the only place in the city where the dead outnumber the living.
