# Lore of the Dwarven Kingdoms

Kingdoms rich in ancient grandeur, unyielding halls carved into the roots of mountains, and the echoing ring of hammers upon anvil—these are the heritage of the Dwarven clans.

### Clan & Honor
Dwarves are defined by their devotion to clan, tradition, and craft. Respect for elders and ancestors runs as deep as the mines they delve. A dwarf's promise is forged in steel; breaking a oath is a shame that tarnishes an entire line for generations.

### Resilience of Stone
Forged by the All-Father, Moradin, dwarves possess stubborn endurance against poison, magic, and hardship. Their memory for stone and metalwork is matchless throughout Faerûn.
