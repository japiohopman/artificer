##Halruaa

Once believed destroyed in the conflagration
of the Spellplague, Halruaa has largely been restored to
the insular, magic-mighty nation it once was. Because
of the foresight of their divinations, Halruaan wizards
were able to use the raging blue fire that followed Mystra's death to propel their nation safely into the realm of
Toril's twin, Abeir (displacing part of that world into the
Plane of Shadow).
Now that the events of those times have mostly been
undone, the famed Halruaan skyships and waterborne vessels have spread out from their home once again,
seeking to establish trading routes and political connections, as well as to learn what has changed of the world
in their century of absence.
