# The Divine Weave: The Gods of the Forgotten Realms

> "The gods do not rule the world; they are the world. Every sunrise is Lathander's promise, every storm is Talos's rage, and every death is Kelemvor's duty." — *Unknown Priest of Oghma*

In the Forgotten Realms, the gods are not distant myths or abstract concepts. They are active, powerful entities whose influence is felt in every aspect of mortal life. From the highest peaks of Celestia to the deepest pits of the Abyss, the pantheon of Faerûn is a complex web of alliances, rivalries, and ancient pacts. This divine structure is overseen by **Ao**, the Overgod, who ensures that the deities fulfill their duties and maintain the balance of the cosmos.

## The Nature of Divinity
Divinity in Faerûn is a matter of both power and responsibility. A god's strength is directly tied to the number and fervor of their worshippers. This creates a dynamic where deities must actively intervene in mortal affairs to prove their worth and maintain their standing. The **Circle of Greater Powers** represents the most influential of these beings, but even the lowliest demigod can shape the fate of a kingdom if their followers are sufficiently dedicated.

## The Portfolio and the Domain
Each deity is defined by their **Portfolio**—the specific aspects of reality they oversee—and their **Domains**, which represent the magical and philosophical spheres through which they manifest their power. For example, **Mystra**, the Goddess of Magic, holds the portfolio of the Weave itself, while **Tempus**, the Lord of Battles, oversees the chaos and honor of war. These roles are not static; through divine conflict and mortal intervention, portfolios can be stolen, traded, or destroyed.

## The Second Sundering and the Modern Pantheon
Following the cataclysmic events of the **Spellplague** and the subsequent **Second Sundering**, the pantheon has returned to a state of relative stability. Many gods who were thought dead or vanished, such as **Bhaal**, **Myrkul**, and **Bane**, have returned to reclaim their ancient seats of power. This "New Pantheon" is a mixture of ancient primordial forces and ascended mortals, all vying for influence in a world that is still healing from its wounds.

## The Mortal Connection
For the people of Faerûn, worship is a practical matter. A sailor prays to **Umberlee** for safe passage, a merchant seeks **Waukeen's** blessing for a profitable trade, and a warrior calls upon **Torm** for the strength to do their duty. The gods are a constant presence, their symbols worn as amulets and their names invoked in both oaths and curses. To live in the Forgotten Realms is to walk in the shadow of the divine, knowing that at any moment, the eyes of a god might turn toward you.
