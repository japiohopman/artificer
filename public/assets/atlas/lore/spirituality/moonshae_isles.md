# The Earth Mother and the Balance: Spirituality of the Isles

The spiritual life of the Moonshae Isles is centered entirely on the maintenance of "The Balance" between the forces of nature.

## The Earth Mother
The Earth Mother is the divine consciousness of the Moonshae Isles. She is not worshipped with grand cathedrals, but through the tending of the land and the protection of the Moonwells. She represents the cycle of birth, growth, and death.
*   **The Three Children:** To maintain the balance of the world, the Earth Mother created three avatars:
    1.  **The Leviathan:** A massive sea creature that protects the waters of the archipelago.
    2.  **The Unicorn (Kamerynn):** The guardian of the forests and the symbol of life's beauty.
    3.  **The Pack (The Ravager):** A manifestation of the necessary violence and death in nature.

## The Moonwells
Moonwells are the most sacred sites in the Moonshaes. They are natural springs that glow with a soft, silvery light, especially under a full moon.
*   **Healing and Magic:** The waters of a Moonwell can heal the sick, restore magic to a wizard, and even provide visions of the future.
*   **The Rusting:** When evil forces corrupt a Moonwell, the water turns into a foul, black sludge. This corruption spreads to the surrounding land, causing plants to die and animals to go mad. Restoring a rusted Moonwell is one of the most heroic deeds a person can perform.

## The Druids
The spiritual leaders of the Folk are the **Druids**. They live in small circles, tending to the Moonwells and advising the local kings. Unlike the clerics of the mainland, their power comes directly from their connection to the Earth Mother and the natural world. They are the keepers of the islands' history and the guardians of the Balance.
