# Horrors of the Dark: A Bestiary of the Underdark

The Underdark is home to creatures that violate the natural laws of the surface world. Many are psionic, others are mutagenic, and all are deadly.

## The Psychic Tyrants

### [[mind_flayer|Mind Flayers (Illithids)]]
Psionic tyrants from another timeline, Mind Flayers reproduce through **ceremorphosis**\u2014consuming a host's brain and transforming them into a new Illithid. They rule through psychic dominance and view all other life as "cattle" for their consumption.

### [[aboleth|Aboleths]]
Ancient, psychic entities that inhabit the sunless seas. They possess the memories of every creature they have ever consumed and manipulate history through subtle telepathic suggestions that can span millennia.

### [[beholder|Beholders]]
Paranoid, many-eyed monsters that can disintegrate matter at will. Their xenophobia is so extreme that they view even other Beholders as threats. They are capable of dreaming reality into new shapes, literally willing new monsters into existence.

## The Predators of the Stone

### [[purple_worm|Purple Worms]]
Apex predators that tunnel through solid rock. Their massive movements determine the fundamental geography of the Underdark, connecting previously isolated cavern systems. Their digestive processes create rich mineral deposits, making their tunnels highly sought after by miners.

### [[tomb_tapper|Tomb Tappers (Thaalud)]]
Fifteen-foot-tall constructs made of living stone that hunt magic. They can sense magical items through miles of rock and will kill anyone to claim an artifact for their hidden hoards.

### [[annihilator|The Annihilator]]
A terrifying mutation of the [[rust_monster|rust monster]]. Instead of rusting metal, it disintegrates both metal and flesh on contact. They are relentless trackers that will pursue a target across continents.

## The Alien and the Aberrant

### [[phthisic|Phthisic]]
Psionic abominations born from failed Mind Flayer experiments. They feed on the life force and sanity of their victims, leaving behind "withered husks" that are neither alive nor truly dead.

### [[ineffable_horror|Ineffable Horror]]
Marauding hunters from the deepest parts of the Lowerdark. They use writhing tentacles that emerge from their stomachs to snare prey and drain their essence, leaving no trace of the victim behind.

### [[vodyanoi|Vodyanoi]]
Aquatic apex predators related to [[umber_hulk|Umber Hulks]]. They inhabit the sunless seas and can summon electric eels to paralyze their prey before dragging them into the cold, crushing depths.
