# The Infernal Legions: A Bestiary of the Damned

The devils of the Nine Hells are not merely monsters; they are the specialized tools of a cosmic machine. Each rank has a purpose, a psychology, and a unique way of inflicting misery.

## The Lowly and the Lost

### Lemures: The Raw Material
Every devil, no matter how powerful, likely began as a lemure. These are the souls of the wicked, stripped of identity and melted into a shapeless mass of quivering, stinking flesh. They are the cannon fodder of the Blood War, mindless and wretched, driven only by a dim memory of pain and a desperate, instinctive urge to obey.

### Imps: The Whispering Shadows
Imps are the eyes and ears of the Hells. Tiny, winged, and capable of turning invisible or shifting into the form of a rat or raven, they are the ultimate spies. They are petty, cruel, and cowardly, delighting in the small-scale corruption of mortals. An imp doesn't want to destroy a kingdom; it wants to convince a priest to steal from the poor box.

## The Iron Soldiers

### Bearded Devils (Barbazu): The Shock Troops
The Barbazu are the backbone of the infernal armies. They are tall, lean, and move with a terrifying, jerky grace. Their most distinctive feature is the mass of writhing, poisonous tentacles that grow from their chins. In battle, they are relentless, their jagged glaives leaving wounds that bleed hellfire and refuse to heal. They do not feel fear; they only feel the cold satisfaction of a command executed.

### Chain Devils (Kytons): The Architects of Agony
A Kyton is a nightmare of cold iron and exposed flesh. They are draped in animate chains that they control with their minds, using them to bind, lash, and flay their victims. They view pain as an art form and themselves as the masters. To look into the eyes of a Chain Devil is to see your own reflection, twisted by the person you fear most.

## The High Command

### Bone Devils (Osyluths): The Infernal Inquisitors
Skeletal, insectile, and towering, the Bone Devils are the police force of the Hells. They have no skin, only a hard, white carapace, and a long, scorpion-like tail tipped with a deadly sting. They are the keepers of records and the enforcers of contracts. If a devil or a mortal attempts to cheat the system, it is the Osyluth who is sent to bring them to "justice."

### Ice Devils (Gelugons): The Cold Strategists
Found primarily in the frozen wastes of Cania, the Gelugons are the masterminds of the infernal legions. They resemble giant, bipedal insects with crystalline carapaces. They feel no emotion—not even the anger or greed of other devils. They see the multiverse as a complex mathematical equation, and they are the variables that will ensure the final result is always in favor of Hell.

### Erinyes: The Fallen Vengeance
The Erinyes are perhaps the most tragic of all devils. They are the descendants of the original angels who fell with Asmodeus. They retain their beauty and their feathered wings, but their hearts are filled with a cold, righteous fury. They are the bounty hunters of the Hells, sent to retrieve souls that have attempted to flee their fate. They do not use fire or poison; they use a magical rope of human hair to bind their prey and drag them back to the pit.

### Pit Fiends: The Lords of Ruin
A Pit Fiend is the pinnacle of the diabolical form. They are massive, winged titans of muscle and malice, wreathed in a permanent aura of fear and fire. A single Pit Fiend can command an entire legion, and their word is law to any lesser devil. They are the generals, the governors, and the ultimate enforcers of Asmodeus's will. To face a Pit Fiend is to face the concentrated essence of the Nine Hells itself.
