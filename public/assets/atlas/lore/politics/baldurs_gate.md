# Power and Steel: The Government of Baldur's Gate

The governance of Baldur's Gate is a delicate balance between the interests of the nobility, the power of the military, and the influence of the merchant class.

## The Council of Four
The city is ruled by four Grand Dukes who form the **Council of Four**. They are elected for life by the Parliament of Peers, though they can be removed for incompetence or treason.
*   **The Grand Duke:** The leader of the council, currently **Ulder Ravengard** (also the Marshal of the Flaming Fist).
*   **The Parliament of Peers:** A legislative body of about fifty influential Baldurians who advise the Council and represent the interests of the patriars and wealthy merchants.

## The Flaming Fist
The **Flaming Fist** is one of the most powerful mercenary companies in Faerûn and serves as the city's standing army and primary law enforcement.
*   **Role:** They maintain order in the Lower City, man the walls, and protect the city's interests abroad.
*   **Reputation:** They are known for their discipline and their willingness to use force to keep the peace. To many citizens, they are the only thing standing between the city and total anarchy.

## The Guild
The true "hidden" government of Baldur's Gate.
*   **The King of Beggars:** The mysterious leader of the Guild, who coordinates the city's criminal activities from the shadows.
*   **Influence:** The Guild has agents in every ward and every level of society. They manage the city's black markets and provide a perverse kind of "order" in the Outer City where the official law does not reach.

## The Dead Three
Baldur's Gate has a dark history with the deities of murder, tyranny, and death—**Bhaal, Bane, and Myrkul**.
*   **Cult Activity:** Secret cults dedicated to the Dead Three have long haunted the city's sewers and basements, their schemes often threatening to plunge the city into a bloodbath. The shadow of these deities is a constant, dark undercurrent in the city's politics.
