# The Masked and the Open: The Government of Waterdeep

The governance of Waterdeep is a unique experiment in anonymous democracy and absolute authority.

## The Lords of Waterdeep
The city is ruled by a council of sixteen Lords, but their identities are one of the greatest secrets in Faerûn.
*   **The Masked Lords:** Fifteen of the Lords remain anonymous. When they meet, they wear magical helms and robes that conceal their height, gender, and voice. This system is designed to prevent bribery, intimidation, and the cult of personality. They are chosen from all walks of life—merchants, laborers, and even reformed criminals.
*   **The Open Lord:** The sixteenth Lord is the only one known to the public. Currently **Laeral Silverhand**, the Open Lord serves as the city's diplomat, commander-in-chief, and public face. While the Open Lord has significant power, they can be overruled by the Masked Lords.

## The Code Legal
Waterdeep's law is codified in a document known as the **Code Legal**. It is strict, comprehensive, and enforced with little room for negotiation.
*   **Magisters (Black Robes):** These are the city's judges. They have the power to hear cases on the spot and issue sentences ranging from fines to death.
*   **The City Watch:** The daily police force. They are generally helpful but strictly enforce the peace.
*   **The City Guard:** The military branch responsible for the city's walls, gates, and the elite **Griffon Cavalry**.

## Major Factions
Power in Waterdeep is a delicate balance between several powerful organizations:
*   **The Harpers:** A secretive network of spies and harbingers of good who work to maintain the balance of power.
*   **The Zhentarim:** Also known as the Black Network, they seek to control the city's trade through shadow and steel.
*   **The Xanathar Guild:** A criminal syndicate led by a paranoid beholder, operating out of the subterranean city of Skullport.
*   **The Lords' Alliance:** A coalition of cities dedicated to mutual defense and trade, with Waterdeep as its primary leader.
*   **The Guilds:** Over 40 official guilds control virtually every aspect of the economy. From the *Guild of Chandlers* to the *Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors*, you cannot do business in Waterdeep without their blessing.
