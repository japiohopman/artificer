# Character Details

Your character is more than a collection of abilities, numbers, and traits.

Soon, the other adventurers at the table will meet them—not through their statistics, but through their **name, appearance, mannerisms, and presence**.

What do they look like? How do they carry themselves? What might someone notice about them the moment they walk into a room?

Your character's physical appearance and personal details are the first pieces of the person you are bringing to life. They can reflect their ancestry, upbringing, profession, travels, or simply the choices they have made along the way.

Think about how these details fit together with the character you have imagined.

A scar might tell the story of a battle. A weathered cloak might hint at years spent on the road. A carefully kept weapon might be a treasured family heirloom—or a reminder of someone who is no longer there.

There are no wrong answers.

These details belong to **your character**, and they are yours to shape.

---

### Bring Your Character to Life

Choose the details that make your adventurer recognizable at the table:

* **Name** — What are they called, and does their name carry any meaning?
* **Appearance** — What do they look like, and what makes them distinctive?
* **Age** — How many years have shaped the person they have become?
* **Height & Build** — How do they physically carry themselves in the world?
* **Eyes, Hair & Features** — What might another adventurer remember after meeting them?
* **Distinguishing Marks** — Scars, tattoos, jewelry, clothing, or other details that tell a story.

Your character sheet records the details.

**Your imagination gives them life.**
