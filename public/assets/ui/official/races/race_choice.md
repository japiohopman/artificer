# Species & Heritage

Your character's species defines their fundamental biological traits, natural talents, and ancestral legacy.

### Considerations
- **Innate Traits:** Species grant unique capabilities such as Darkvision, elemental resistances, or innate spellcasting.
- **Speed & Size:** Size impacts tactical movement and space, while Speed determines base tactical movement on the grid.
- **Languages:** Your species dictates your native tongues and ancestral knowledge.
