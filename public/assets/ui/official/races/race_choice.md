# Choosing Your Species

In the world of Dungeons & Dragons, your species (or ancestry) represents your character's mortal heritage and natural biological gifts.

---

### What Species Defines
* **Biological Traits:** Innate capabilities such as Darkvision, natural resistances, or magical traits.
* **Ability Score Increases:** Physical and mental inclinations passed down through generations.
* **Size & Speed:** Physical stature and tactical mobility on the battlefield.
* **Ancestral Culture:** Languages spoken and traditions carried through the ages.
