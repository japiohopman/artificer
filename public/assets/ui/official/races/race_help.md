# Species & Lineage Help

Choosing a species determines your character's foundational physical traits and heritage.

---

### Key Considerations for Beginners
* **Synergy with Class:** Some species offer ability bonuses that naturally complement certain classes (e.g., Elves with Dexterity for Rogues/Rangers, Dwarves with Constitution/Strength for Fighters).
* **Innate Features:** Look at traits like Darkvision (seeing in lightless environments) or resistance to damage types.
* **Flavor & Identity:** Pick a species whose roleplay and backstory excite you most!
