# Species Guidance & D&D Rules

Species choices in Dungeons & Dragons establish your character's physiological foundation.

### Core Mechanics
1. **Size:** Most playable species are Medium or Small. Medium creatures occupy a 5 ft grid space, while Small creatures can squeeze into tighter passages.
2. **Speed:** Base walking speed measures how far you can travel in a single movement action (typically 25 ft to 35 ft).
3. **Traits & Resistances:**
   - **Darkvision:** Allows seeing in dim light as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.
   - **Resistances:** Reduces damage taken from specific energy types (e.g., Poison for Dwarves, Fire for Tieflings) by half.
   - **Subraces:** Specialized lineages within a species providing tailored ability bonuses and unique features.
