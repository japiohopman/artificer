# Choose Your Equipment

Every adventurer needs the right tools for the journey ahead.

Your **class and background** determine the equipment you begin your adventure with, from weapons and armor to useful tools, supplies, and other pieces of adventuring gear. These items are the things your character relies upon when the road becomes dangerous.

Record your starting equipment on your character sheet.

---

## Take Your Starting Gear

The simplest way to prepare for your adventure is to accept the equipment provided by your class and background.

This gives you everything you need to begin your journey without having to worry about the cost of each individual item.

But perhaps your character has different needs—or simply different tastes.

### Build Your Own Kit

Instead of taking the equipment provided by your class and background, you can purchase your own starting gear using **gold pieces (gp)**.

Spend your starting gold wisely.

A sturdy sword might keep you alive in a fight, but a rope, torch, bedroll, or set of tools could prove just as valuable when the path ahead takes an unexpected turn.

Choose the equipment that fits **the adventurer you have imagined**.

---

## Don't Forget the Little Things

You may also choose **one trinket** at no cost.

It might be something strange, sentimental, mysterious, or completely ordinary—a small object that has somehow found its way into your character's possession.

Perhaps it has a story.

Perhaps it will have one someday.

---

## Pack Wisely

Every adventurer has limits.

Your **Strength score** determines how much equipment you can reasonably carry. As a general rule, try to keep the total weight of your equipment within **15 pounds for every point of Strength** you possess.

A heavily equipped warrior may have little trouble carrying a suit of armor and a collection of weapons. A lightly built wizard may need to think more carefully about what comes along for the journey.

Remember:

**The best adventurers prepare for the unexpected—but they don't carry the entire world on their backs.**

---

### Ready Your Pack

Choose your weapons.

Gather your armor.

Pack your tools and supplies.

Take one small thing worth keeping.

**Your adventure is about to begin.**
