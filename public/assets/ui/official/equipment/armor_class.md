# Armor Class

When danger strikes, what stands between your character and a blade?

Your **Armor Class (AC)** represents how difficult your character is to hit in combat. It reflects more than the armor you wear—it can include your agility, reflexes, a shield, protective magic, or simply the ability to move out of harm's way at exactly the right moment.

A heavily armored warrior may withstand a blow behind a wall of steel. A nimble rogue might simply step aside before the blade ever finds its mark.

Both can be equally difficult to hit.

---

## What Determines Your AC?

Several things can contribute to your Armor Class:

* **Armor** — The protection provided by the armor you wear.
* **Shield** — A shield can make you harder to strike by deflecting blows and protecting vulnerable areas.
* **Dexterity** — Quick reflexes and agility can help you avoid attacks, particularly when wearing little or no armor.
* **Magic & Class Features** — Certain spells, abilities, and special traits can provide alternative ways to determine your AC.

If your character wears no armor and carries no shield, their Armor Class is normally:

**10 + your Dexterity modifier**

If you wear armor, carry a shield, or both, your equipment determines how your AC is calculated.

---

## Proficiency Matters

Knowing how to wear armor is very different from simply putting it on.

Your **class** determines which types of armor and shields your character is proficient with. Proficiency represents the training required to move, fight, and perform effectively while using that equipment.

Wearing armor or carrying a shield without the proper proficiency can interfere with your abilities and prevent you from using it effectively.

Choose your protection carefully—and make sure your character knows how to use it.

---

## Not All Defense Comes From Steel

Armor isn't the only way to survive a dangerous encounter.

Some **spells, class features, and special abilities** provide their own methods for determining your Armor Class. If your character has more than one ability that gives you a different way to calculate your AC, you choose which calculation to use.

Your defense might come from a suit of enchanted plate.

It might come from a shield raised at exactly the right moment.

Or perhaps your character is simply **too quick to be touched**.

Record your **Armor Class** on your character sheet.
