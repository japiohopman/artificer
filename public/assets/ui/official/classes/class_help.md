# Classes

Adventurers are extraordinary people.

Driven by curiosity, ambition, duty, or simply a thirst for something beyond ordinary life, they choose a path that most people would never dare to walk. They venture into forgotten ruins, confront monsters in the darkness, challenge powerful enemies, and step willingly into dangers that lesser folk would flee from.

At the heart of every adventurer is their **class**.

Your class is more than a profession. It is your character's **calling**—the discipline they have devoted themselves to, the talents they have cultivated, and the way they have learned to face the world.

A fighter may see the world through the eyes of strategy and battlefield tactics, always searching for the next advantage. A cleric may see themselves as a servant of a greater divine purpose, caught in a struggle between powers far beyond the mortal world.

A rogue might have contacts among thieves, merchants, and other underworld figures. A druid may know the secrets of ancient groves and the creatures that dwell within them. A wizard may spend their life among scholars and seekers of forgotten knowledge.

Your class shapes not only **what you can do**, but also how you understand the world—and your place within it.

---

## The Classes

Each class grants you a unique collection of abilities and proficiencies.

At lower levels, you may have only a handful of class features. As you gain experience and advance in level, your abilities grow stronger, new features become available, and your existing talents may improve.

Choose a class that matches the kind of adventurer you want to become.

## Growing Into Your Class

Your class is not something you simply select once and leave behind.

As you adventure, your character grows into their chosen path. New abilities become available, old talents become stronger, and the choices you make along the way can shape the kind of hero you ultimately become.

A fighter might become a legendary master of the battlefield. A wizard could uncover spells lost for centuries. A rogue might rise from a common thief to one of the most capable infiltrators in the realm.

**Your first level is only the beginning.**

---

## More Than One Path

Not every adventurer follows a single path for their entire life.

A rogue might discover a higher purpose and swear the sacred oath of a paladin. A barbarian may uncover a latent magical gift and begin studying the powers of the sorcerer while continuing to embrace their primal fury.

Some elves are renowned for combining martial discipline with arcane study, walking the paths of both **fighter and wizard**.

Rules that allow a character to advance in more than one class are known as **multiclassing**.

Your character's journey does not always have to follow a straight road.

---

## Find Your Calling

The twelve classes represent the most common paths taken by adventurers throughout the worlds of Dungeons & Dragons.

Each offers a different way to experience the game, overcome challenges, and contribute to an adventuring party.

**Choose the path that calls to you.**

The world is waiting to discover what kind of hero you will become.
