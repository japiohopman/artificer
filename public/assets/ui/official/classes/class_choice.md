# Choosing Your Class

Every adventurer has a **class**. Your class describes your character's vocation, training, and the special talents they have developed. It shapes the way they approach danger, the tactics they favor, and the role they play when exploring ancient ruins, fighting monsters, or navigating a tense negotiation.

Where your species tells the story of **where you come from**, your class begins to define **what you have learned to do**.

Will you stand on the front line as a mighty warrior? Master the arcane arts as a wizard? Walk unseen through the shadows as a rogue? Or call upon divine power as a cleric?

The choice is yours.

---

## What Your Class Gives You

Your class provides many of the abilities and proficiencies that define your character throughout their adventuring career.

### Class Features

Your class grants you unique **class features**—special capabilities that distinguish you from members of other classes.

These may include powerful combat abilities, extraordinary talents, or the ability to **cast spells** and wield magic.

As you gain levels, your class continues to unlock new abilities and opportunities, allowing your character to grow from an inexperienced adventurer into a seasoned hero.

### Proficiencies

Your class also grants various **proficiencies**, representing the skills and training your character has acquired.

These may include:

* **Armor** — the protection you know how to wear and use effectively.
* **Weapons** — the weapons you have been trained to wield.
* **Skills** — areas of expertise that come naturally through your training.
* **Saving Throws** — abilities you rely upon when resisting dangerous effects.
* **Tools** — specialized equipment with which you have learned to work.

Your proficiencies define many of the things your character can do particularly well—from striking with a trusted weapon to tracking a creature through the wilderness or telling a convincing lie.

Record all the features and proficiencies your class grants you at **1st level** on your character sheet.

# Your First Level

Most characters begin their adventuring career at **1st level**.

At this stage, your character may be inexperienced in the world of adventuring, even if they have already lived a dangerous life. A former soldier, sailor, criminal, or hunter may have faced plenty of danger before—but becoming an adventurer marks the beginning of something new.

**1st level is where your story begins.**

As you adventure, overcome challenges, and survive encounters, you gain **experience points (XP)** and advance in level.

Each new level represents growth: greater skill, deeper knowledge, stronger abilities, and new challenges worthy of a more experienced hero.

If you are joining an existing campaign, or already know the game well, your Dungeon Master may choose to begin your character at a higher level. In that case, your character has already survived adventures that would test an inexperienced hero.

Record your **level** and **experience points** on your character sheet.

A 1st-level character begins with **0 XP**. Characters beginning at higher levels typically start with the minimum XP required to reach that level.

# Hit Points & Hit Dice

Adventuring is dangerous.

Your **hit points (HP)** represent how much punishment your character can withstand before falling in battle or becoming unable to continue.

Your hit points are determined by your **Hit Dice**—short for *Hit Point Dice*—which are tied to your class.

A sturdy warrior may be able to withstand blows that would bring down a fragile spellcaster, while a nimble rogue may rely on speed and cunning to avoid taking those blows in the first place.

Your Hit Dice are therefore more than another number on your character sheet.

They are part of what determines **how you survive when the adventure turns deadly**.
