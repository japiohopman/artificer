# Choose Your Calling

Your class defines your primary archetype, combat role, and special abilities in the Atlas.

### Calling Breakdown
- **Martial Combatants:** Fighters, Barbarians, and Paladins excel in weapon mastery and frontline defense.
- **Spellcasters:** Wizards, Clerics, and Sorcerers command magical weave energy to heal, destroy, or control the battlefield.
- **Skilled Specialists:** Rogues, Bards, and Rangers combine mobility, subterfuge, and specialized skills.
