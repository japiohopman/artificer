# Choosing Your Class

Your class is the primary defining aspect of your adventurer—their vocation, special training, and tactical combat focus.

---

### What Class Defines
* **Combat & Arcana Capabilities:** Martial prowess, spellcasting abilities, or sacred channeling.
* **Hit Die & Resilience:** Your base health pool and durability in battle.
* **Saving Throws & Proficiencies:** Weapons, armor, and saving throw specialties.
* **Path of Progression:** Features and abilities unlocked as your character gains experience.

Select a class from the records on the left to examine their abilities and hit dice.
