# Charlatan

You have an instinct for people and a talent for deception, which you used to make your way in the world. You know how to read what others desire, fear, or envy, and you spin stories or assumed identities to exploit those feelings.

Perhaps you were a street-corner con artist selling fake remedies, a high-stakes gambler with loaded dice, or an impostor posing as noble aristocracy. You live by your wits and misdirection.

---

## Schemes & Disguises

For a Charlatan, identity is a canvas. You master the arts of camouflage, vocal mimicry, forged credentials, and persuasive patter to slip through life undetected.

Your schemes may have been born of desperation or greed, but in the life of an adventurer, your quick thinking and ability to deceive enemies can save your companions from dire peril.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma
* **Origin Feat:** Skilled
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Sleight of Hand
* **Tool Proficiency:** Disguise Kit
* **Starting Equipment:** Disguise Kit, Fine Clothes, Pouch, and 15 GP.
