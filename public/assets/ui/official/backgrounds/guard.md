# Guard

You served as a sentry, city watch officer, jailer, or mercenary guard. Your duty was to enforce the law, protect property, and defend gates or garrisons against intruders and criminals.

You spent long hours on watch, training with weapons, inspecting passersby, and staying vigilant against danger. You know how guards think and how patrols operate.

---

## Vigilance & Order

Guards know the importance of alertness. You are trained to spot subtle threats, respond quickly when alarms sound, and maintain discipline in tense situations.

Your military training and martial readiness give you an edge when combat begins, allowing you to react swiftly to protect your companions.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom
* **Origin Feat:** Alert
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics, Perception
* **Tool Proficiency:** Gaming Set (One of your choice)
* **Starting Equipment:** Gaming Set (same as choice), Light Crossbow, 20 Crossbow Bolts, Spear, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 12 GP.
