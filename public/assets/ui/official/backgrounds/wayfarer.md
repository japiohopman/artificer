# Wayfarer

You grew up on the streets of a crowded city or as a wanderer along trade roads, surviving by your wits, luck, and agility. You had no wealth or high social status to protect you.

You know how to navigate city alleys, find secret passages, dodge corrupt guards, and survive in the harsh realities of urban sprawl or road life.

---

## Street Smarts & Luck

Wayfarers are adaptable and quick-footed. You know how to Blend into crowds, pick locks, spot pickpockets, and rely on your instincts when danger approaches.

Your resourceful nature and street smarts give you the ability to turn disadvantage into opportunity when adventuring through hostile lands.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma
* **Origin Feat:** Lucky
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Stealth
* **Tool Proficiency:** Thieves' Tools
* **Starting Equipment:** Thieves' Tools, 2 Daggers, Map Case, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 16 GP.
