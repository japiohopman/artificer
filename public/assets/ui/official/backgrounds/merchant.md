# Merchant

You worked in the world of commerce, trading goods and services across markets, caravans, and shipping ports. You learned how supply and demand drive economies, how to bargain, and how to manage ledger books.

You have an eye for value and an understanding of human desires. You know how to negotiate deals, establish trade contacts, and spot opportunities for profit.

---

## Trade & Negotiation

Merchants know how to navigate social settings and financial markets. Whether trading spices, textiles, livestock, or weapons, you understand contract law, currencies, and maritime trade routes.

Your sharp mind and knack for negotiation can secure better prices, valuable information, and influential contacts for your adventuring company.
