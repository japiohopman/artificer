# Background & Origin Help

Your background roots your character in the world, providing context for why they became an adventurer.

---

### Background Benefits
* **Skills & Proficiencies:** Backgrounds grant skill proficiencies that expand your capabilities outside your class.
* **Features:** Unique benefits (e.g., Acolyte's Shelter of the Faithful or Noble's Position of Privilege).
* **Starting Gear:** Adventuring gear and gold tailored to your former trade.
