# Background Rules & Lore Guide

Backgrounds connect your character's stats to their narrative history.

### What Your Background Provides
1. **Proficiencies:** Skills granted by your background combine with your class proficiencies to determine your d20 skill bonuses.
2. **Special Features:** Unique benefits like an Acolyte's *Shelter of the Faithful* or a Folk Hero's *Rustic Hospitality*.
3. **Equipment & Wealth:** Starting gear from your background is added to your inventory alongside your class gear.
