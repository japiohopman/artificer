# Artisan

You began your life as an apprentice to a master crafter, spending long hours in a workshop or guildhall learning a trade. Through disciplined practice, you learned to turn raw materials into goods of beauty, utility, and value.

Your background gives you a practical understanding of trade, craftsmanship, and trade guilds. You know the effort required to forge armor, weave textiles, build furniture, or craft fine glassware, and you respect those who work with their hands.

---

## Craft & Commerce

Artisans are the backbone of civilization's economy. Whether you forged weapons in a dwarven stronghold or crafted delicate jewelry in a bustling metropolis, your skill with tools sets you apart.

You know how to inspect goods for quality, estimate trade value, and navigate the networks of merchants and guild masters that span towns and cities.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence
* **Origin Feat:** Crafter
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Investigation, Persuasion
* **Tool Proficiency:** Artisan's Tools (One of your choice)
* **Starting Equipment:** Artisan's Tools (same as choice), Abacus, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 23 GP.
