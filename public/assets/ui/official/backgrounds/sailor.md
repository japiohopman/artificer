# Sailor

You spent your formative years aboard a seagoing ship, facing howling gales, crashing waves, pirate raids, and sea monsters. You know how to haul rigging, tie knots, trim sails, and navigate by the stars.

The sea is in your blood. You feel at home on the deck of a ship and in coastal port taverns where sailors gather to trade tales of the sea.

---

## Life at Sea

Sailors are tough, physical, and team-oriented. Working a ship requires coordination, physical strength, and quick reflexes when storms strike.

Your experience at sea gives you martial toughness and an understanding of nautical navigation, weather patterns, and maritime lore.

---

* **Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom
