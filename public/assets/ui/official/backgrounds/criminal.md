# Criminal

You have a history of breaking the law and surviving in the underworld. You grew up or operated in the shadows of society, relying on stealth, lockpicking, and street smarts to stay alive.

Whether you were a burglar infiltrating noble manors, a highway robber on trade roads, or a fence dealing in stolen goods, you know how criminal networks operate and how to avoid the local guard.

---

## Underworld Smart & Tactics

Criminals know that the law is enforced by guards, but governed by opportunity. You understand the unspoken rules of thieves' guilds, black markets, and illicit syndicates.

Your instincts for stealth and keen awareness of traps and ambushes make you invaluable when creeping through dark dungeons and hostile territory.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence
* **Origin Feat:** Alert
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Stealth
* **Tool Proficiency:** Thieves' Tools
* **Starting Equipment:** Thieves' Tools, Crowbar, 2 Daggers, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 16 GP.
