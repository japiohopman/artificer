# Guide

You came of age in the wilderness, leading travelers, caravans, and explorers through dangerous terrain. You know how to read animal tracks, navigate uncharted forests, cross mountains, and survive natural hazards.

The wild frontier was your home. You learned to move quietly through brush, find fresh water, and avoid predatory beasts and hostile monsters.

---

## Wilderness Survival

Guides possess an intimate knowledge of the land and its hazards. You know which berries are safe to eat, how to build shelters from branches, and how to stay on course by the stars or landmark terrain.

Your ability to scout ahead and keep your party safe in untamed regions makes you essential to any expedition into the unknown.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
* **Origin Feat:** Magic Initiate (Druid)
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Stealth, Survival
* **Tool Proficiency:** Cartographer's Tools
* **Starting Equipment:** Cartographer's Tools, Bedroll, Map Case, Tent, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 2 GP.
