# Character Origins & Background

Your background reflects where you came from, how you became an adventurer, and your place in the world.

### Benefits Granted
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Two dedicated skills reflective of your upbringing.
- **Background Feature:** A unique social privilege or practical benefit in civilized or wild lands.
- **Starting Equipment:** Essential gear, tools, and gold pieces representing your prior profession.
- **Languages / Tools:** Unique linguistic abilities or artisan tool proficiencies.
