# Entertainer

You thrive in front of an audience. You spent your youth learning how to captivate crowds through music, poetry, acrobatics, storytelling, or acting.

You know how to entrance people, lift their spirits with song, or distract them with drama. To you, the world is a stage, and every tavern or hall is an opportunity to perform.

---

## Performance & Presence

Entertainers know how to read the mood of a room. Whether singing epic ballads of ancient heroes or tumbling across a town square, you know how to win the favor of commoners and nobles alike.

Your theatrical flair and musical talent allow you to inspire your allies and secure food, lodging, or information wherever you travel.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Charisma
* **Origin Feat:** Musician
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Acrobatics, Performance
* **Tool Proficiency:** Musical Instrument (One of your choice)
* **Starting Equipment:** Musical Instrument (same as choice), 2 Costumes, Perfume, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 8 GP.
