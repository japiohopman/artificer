# Noble

You were born into a family of high social standing, wealth, and political influence. You grew up in manors or castles, educated in history, etiquette, governance, and high society customs.

You carry a family title and coat of arms. People of lower station are accustomed to treating you with respect, and you are comfortable moving through noble courts and diplomatic gatherings.

---

## Privilege & Governance

Nobles understand the games of power and lineage. You know how feudal estates operate, how monarchs rule, and how aristocratic alliances are forged and broken.

Your education and silver tongue allow you to sway high-ranking officials and negotiate with diplomats, knights, and merchant lords.
