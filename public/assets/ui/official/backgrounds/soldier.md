# Soldier

War has been your life for as long as you can remember. You trained in military tactics, drilled with weapons, and served in an army, mercenary company, or city garrison.

You know the discipline of military life, the chaos of the battlefield, and the bond formed between comrades under fire. You carry military rank or the veteran instincts of combat.

---

## Military Discipline & Warfare

Soldiers understand military hierarchy, unit formations, and battlefield logistics. You know how to march long distances, maintain your arms and armor, and execute tactical maneuvers.

Your military training gives you physical toughness and aggressive combat instincts that serve you well in life-or-death battles.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution
* **Origin Feat:** Savage Attacker
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics, Intimidation
