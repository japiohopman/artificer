# Sage

You spent years studying the lore of the multiverse in academies, libraries, or arcana towers. You devoted your mind to manuscripts, historical chronicles, astrological charts, and magical treatises.

Your thirst for knowledge drove you to research ancient civilizations, forgotten gods, planar realms, and arcane mysteries. To you, knowledge is the greatest power in existence.

---

## Research & Lore

Sages are scholars and historians. When faced with an unknown riddle, magical glyph, or ancient ruin, you know how to analyze clues and cross-reference information to uncover the truth.

Your scholarly training makes you the party's primary authority on history, magical phenomena, and planar cosmology.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom
* **Origin Feat:** Magic Initiate (Wizard)
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, History
* **Tool Proficiency:** Calligrapher's Supplies
* **Starting Equipment:** Calligrapher's Supplies, Book (History), Bottle of Ink, Ink Pen, Parchment (8 sheets), Robes, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 8 GP.
