# Acolyte

You devoted your early years to religious service in a temple, shrine, or sacred sanctuary. Under the guidance of priests, you performed sacred rites, maintained holy relics, offered sacrifices, and studied the teachings of your pantheon or deity.

Whether you served a benevolent deity of light and healing or a dark power of storms and conquest, your time in the temple shaped your worldview and granted you a deep connection to the divine forces that weave through the multiverse.

---

## Devotion & Sanctuaries

As an Acolyte, you were trained in religious traditions and the lore of holy scriptures. Temples are places of community, refuge, and sacred power, and your background gives you familiarity with religious hierarchy and rituals.

You know how to conduct prayers, offer blessings, and interpret holy symbols. Fellow believers and members of your faith recognize your dedication, offering fellowship and sanctuary in established holy sites.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma
