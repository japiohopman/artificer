# Scribe

You earned your living by copying manuscripts, recording official court documents, drafting contracts, and illustrating illumination texts for scholars, nobles, or temple archives.

You have a meticulous eye for detail, handwriting styles, and legal phrasing. You spend hours with pen and ink, carefully preserving knowledge and recording history.

---

## Documents & Recordkeeping

Scribes are masters of written communication. You know how to examine documents for forgery, draft clear reports, and organize vast amounts of information into legible records.

Your sharp eyes and attention to detail help you spot hidden clues, cipher messages, and subtle details that others overlook.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom
