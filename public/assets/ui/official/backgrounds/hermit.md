# Hermit

You lived in seclusion for a formative period of your life, dwelling in a remote cave, mountain monastery, quiet grove, or solitary tower away from the distractions of society.

In the silence of your solitude, you pondered the mysteries of the multiverse, studied ancient texts, or sought spiritual enlightenment through meditation and quiet communion with nature.

---

## Contemplation & Healing

Hermits develop inner discipline and deep insight. Removed from political intrigue and city life, you learned to prepare natural remedies and herbal medicine to treat injuries and ailments.

Your period of contemplation granted you unique perspective and spiritual fortitude that guides your decisions on your adventures.

---

## Origin Features & Mechanics

* **Ability Scores:** Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
* **Origin Feat:** Healer
* **Skill Proficiencies:** Medicine, Religion
* **Tool Proficiency:** Herbalism Kit
* **Starting Equipment:** Herbalism Kit, Bedroll, Book (Philosophy or Scripture), Lamp, 3 Flasks of Oil, Traveler's Clothes, Pouch, and 16 GP.
